# crazyflow 0.3.2 → 0.4.0: re-validation of the RPM experts (2026-10-29, seed 0, RTX 2080)

All numbers are measured on the same laptop as `2026-09-08_seed0.md` (tier-1): 1,024 fresh tracks
per cell, all gates required, deterministic policy, domain randomisation (DR) on unless stated,
evaluation seed 0. `scripts/eval_matrix.py` runs every cell with exactly the settings of the
2026-09-08 tables and of the heatmap. The 2026-09-08 cells reproduce bit-for-bit on 0.3.2 (checked
on four cells), so every difference below comes from the simulator or from retraining.

## Summary

- **The 0.3.2 checkpoints do not transfer to 0.4.0.** `five_gates` and `mixed_tracks` lose 41–87
  points on every cell that was above 50 %; `three_gates` loses 13–24 points.
- **The cause is the new rotor-command clip.** 0.4.0 clips the commanded RPM to the motor limits
  (0.0128–0.12 N per motor, i.e. 8,398–23,216 RPM = 0.44–1.21 × hover for `cf2x_L250`). The 0.3.2
  `mixed_tracks` expert commands more than that in 47–58 % of all motor commands. Without the clip,
  0.4.0 gives the 0.3.2 results back (ablation below).
- **Retrained on 0.4.0** (same curriculum, same configs, 5.0 min), `five_gates` matches or beats
  its 0.3.2 numbers on chains. `mixed_tracks` is 0–7 points below its 0.3.2 numbers on the main
  cells (better on 90° circles), and it flies about 15 % slower. A second seed and a 60 M-step fine-tune do not close the
  gap (side runs below).
- The README now presents the retrained 0.4.0 checkpoints (`outputs/*/2026-10-29_*`).

## What changed in 0.4.0 for this repository

Read from the diff of the installed 0.3.2 and 0.4.0 packages.

| Change | Effect here |
|---|---|
| Rotor command clipped to `[rpm(thrust_min), rpm(thrust_max)]` in the step pipeline (`clip_rotor_vel_cmd`) | **Breaks the 0.3.2 checkpoints.** The action mapping reaches 1.35 × hover (1.42 × with the +5 % motor-gain DR); the clip stops at 1.21 × hover. Max thrust-to-weight 1.49. The env now clips too, so `info["rpm_cmd"]` is the executed command. |
| `cf2x_L250` parameters moved to `dynamics/first_principles/params.toml`: mass 0.0319 → 0.0328 kg; drag xy −0.01472 → −0.01495, z −0.01278 → −0.01390 N/(m/s) | Hover 18,968 → 19,217 RPM. Harmless on its own (inside the ±10 % mass DR, see ablation). |
| Params have no world axis by default (`mass` (1,), `J` (3, 3)); every param accepts leading (world, drone) axes; thrust/torque curves and rotor coefficients also accept a motor axis | `init_state` now broadcasts `mass`, `J`, `J_inv` to a world axis so that the state keeps static shapes through reset and `lax.scan`. |
| `load_params(fn, drone)` → `load_params(Dynamics, drone)` / `load_fn_params(fn, drone)`; `first_principles.sim_dynamics` and `SimData.states_deriv` removed; `Drone` enum | `env.py` calls `load_params(Dynamics.first_principles, drone)`. Strings still work for `drone`. |
| `q_dot` fixed to the xyzw convention | No effect: explicit Euler integrates the body rates directly and discards `quat_dot`. |
| Semi-implicit Euler fixed | Not used (default explicit Euler). |
| Buffer donation for `sim.data` | Only prepares for it: each `SimState` field has its own buffer now. Nothing in crazyflow donates, and `env.py` calls the step function inside its own `jit`, so no donated buffer is reused. |
| Body-rate controller, Holybro X500, sharding and memory changes, `contacts()` returns per-slot flags | Not used. |
| Ground effect / downwash (release notes) | No code for them in the 0.4.0 wheel, so they are off. |

`render.py` works unchanged (`sim.spec`, `build_mjx`, `render(camera="fpv_cam:0")`; gymnasium
1.3 → 1.4).

## Ablation: which change breaks the 0.3.2 checkpoints

0.3.2 checkpoints on 0.4.0 with the clip removed and/or the 0.3.2 mass and drag restored
(1,024 tracks per cell).

| Cell | 0.3.2 | 0.4.0 | 0.4.0, no clip | 0.4.0, 0.3.2 mass + drag | 0.4.0, no clip + 0.3.2 mass + drag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `five_gates`, chain 5 | 0.957 | 0.242 | 0.952 | 0.339 | 0.957 |
| `mixed_tracks`, mix 5 | 0.967 | 0.221 | 0.969 | 0.333 | 0.967 |
| `mixed_tracks`, full circle 8 | 0.971 | 0.112 | 0.975 | 0.218 | 0.971 |

With both changes reverted, 0.4.0 reproduces 0.3.2 exactly, so no other change in 0.4.0 affects
this task.

How often the `mixed_tracks` experts hit the limit: saved trajectories of 256 tracks (0.3.2) and
64 tracks (0.4.0), all steps of all episodes; speed and lap time over the successful episodes.

| Expert | Track | Motor commands ≥ 23,216 RPM | Control steps with any motor there | Mean speed | Lap time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.3.2 expert on 0.3.2 | full circle 8 | 58 % | 75 % | 4.39 m/s | 3.23 s |
| 0.3.2 expert on 0.3.2 | mix 5 | 47 % | 63 % | – | – |
| retrained on 0.4.0 | full circle 8 | 30 % (clipped) | 58 % | 3.78 m/s | 3.75 s |
| retrained on 0.4.0 | chain 5 | 21 % (clipped) | 43 % | – | – |

The retrained expert still spends a large share of the time at full thrust; above `a ≈ 0.6`
(0.46–0.74 with the motor-gain DR) the action has no effect. A mapping that ends at the motor limit would remove that dead zone (not done
here: it changes the action space of the RPM baseline).

## Retraining on 0.4.0

Same commands and overrides as the 2026-09-08 runs (`experiment=three_gates`, then `five_gates` and
`mixed_tracks` with `run.init_from`), seed 0.

chains (0.452 vs 0.901 on 10 gates); `five_gates` repairs that, as before.

### Side runs: is the `mixed_tracks` gap seed noise?

Only the last stage was varied, warm-started from the same 0.4.0 `five_gates` checkpoint. Eval
JSONs in `crazyflow040/side_runs/` (checkpoints not committed).

| Cell | seed 0, 30 M (README) | seed 1, 30 M | seed 0, 60 M | 0.3.2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mix 5 | 0.944 | 0.970 | 0.953 | 0.967 |
| mix 10 | 0.854 | 0.872 | 0.846 | 0.891 |
| full circle 8 | 0.926 | 0.934 | 0.876 | 0.971 |
| circle 90°/gate | 0.667 | 0.662 | 0.691 | 0.482 |
| plain chain 5 | 0.943 | 0.955 | 0.964 | 0.966 |
| plain chain 10 | 0.829 | 0.861 | 0.874 | 0.897 |

Seed-to-seed spread of the last stage is about 1–3 points. The 0.4.0 expert stays below 0.3.2 on
the full circles and on 10-gate tracks in all three runs, and a longer fine-tune does not fix it.
That the thrust cap is the cause is likely (it is the only change that matters for the 0.3.2
checkpoints, and the expert is slower) but not tested directly.
